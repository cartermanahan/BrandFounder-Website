Before you ask

Only the useful bits.

Is this actually generated inside the app?

The example directions and outputs were generated through BrandFounder. Promotional and device scenes are mock-ups used to show how those demo assets could look in context.

What do I get for £17.99?

The UK Brand Pack is a £17.99 one-time in-app purchase. Regional App Store pricing may vary, and the price Apple shows before purchase is the price that applies.

What is actually inside the brand pack?

Brand strategy, logo and visual assets, messaging, launch checklist, 30-day posting schedule, ad scripts, and a full ZIP export.

Do I need design or branding experience?

No. You provide the brief, then review and refine the generated direction before using it.

Who is BrandFounder for?

Founders, freelancers, creators, and solo builders who want a brand pack without spreading the work across multiple tools and freelancers.

What happens if generation fails after purchase?

An interrupted purchase can be retried without a second charge where the entitlement remains available. Contact support if generation or delivery does not recover.

Can I restore purchases?

Yes. Tap restore purchases in the app and your App Store state syncs back.

Who handles billing and refunds?

Apple processes App Store billing and refund requests under its policies; statutory consumer rights are unaffected. Contact BrandFounder support for generation or delivery problems.

Do you store my brand data?

Yes — so your projects stay accessible across sessions and devices when you are signed in.

Do you collect analytics or review my inputs?

Optional product analytics is off unless you enable it. Inputs needed for an AI feature are still processed when you request that feature after the in-app disclosure. Optional sharing to improve results is separate and is also off by default.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Permanent account deletion is available in the app. The Privacy Policy lists the account data the deletion service removes and the limited lawful exceptions.

Is macOS available?

Not yet. iOS is live now. macOS is planned.

How long does generation take?

Generation normally takes minutes, but timing varies with request complexity, connectivity and provider availability.
